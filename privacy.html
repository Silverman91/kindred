Silverman's Kindred Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Silverman's Kindred is a small independent game by Silverman91. This page explains what information the game uses, on the website (https://silverman91.github.io/kindred/) and in the Android app.

The short version

  • You don't need an account. We never ask for your name, email or any personal details.
  • Your progress, best runs and settings stay on your own device.
  • The Android app may show an optional video ad when you choose "watch an ad to continue". Those ads are provided by Google AdMob, which collects some device information to show and measure ads.
  • The website version shows no ads.

Information stored on your device

The game saves a few things in your device's local storage so it can remember them next time: your best runs, the shadows you've met, and your sound and display settings. This information never leaves your device and we can't see it. Uninstalling the app or clearing the browser's site data deletes it.

Ads in the Android app

Some versions of the Android app offer a rewarded video ad after you lose a run. Ads only play when you tap "watch an ad to continue". They're served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

InformationWhy
Your device's advertising IDShowing ads, measuring them, and preventing fraud
General device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version)Showing ads that work on your device
Approximate location based on your IP addressShowing ads that fit your region
How you interact with adsMeasuring ads and preventing fraud

Google handles this information under its own policies: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset your advertising ID or turn off personalized ads in your Android settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads, depending on your phone).

Internet use

The game loads its font from Google Fonts when you're online, and the Android app connects to Google AdMob to load ads. Apart from that, the game doesn't send any information to us or to anyone else.

Children

Kindred is meant for a general audience and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy in Kindred: please open an issue at github.com/Silverman91/kindred/issues.
